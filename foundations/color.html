Contrast pairs

WCAG 2.2 AA needs 4.5:1 for body text and 3:1 for large text and UI parts. Lime is bright enough that it only works as a background behind black text.

Usage

[ Do ]

  • Keep pages white and separate sections with 1px hairlines.
  • Use ink for text and primary actions; grey for supporting copy.
  • Use lime once or twice per page: the marquee band and one accent button.

[ Don’t ]

  • Set text in lime on white (1.3:1).
  • Use Grey 400 for body text (3.5:1).
  • Use pink and green outside the marquee bands.

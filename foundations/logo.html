The mark

A smile made from the :) I sign off with, turned on its side and set in a lime circle. It sits next to “Chay”, the name friends and teammates use.

The wordmark is set in Outfit Medium and outlined as vectors. Ink and lime are the only colors, so it works on every page of the system.

Versions

Color, black, reverse and white versions, plus the mark on its own — every file is an SVG.

Favicon & app icons

The ink tile keeps the lime smile visible on both light and dark browser tabs. This site uses it — check your tab.

Clear space & sizing

  • Clear space: half the smile’s diameter on every side.
  • Minimum size: 96px wide (logo), 24px (mark), 16px (favicon).
  • Alt text: “Chay”. Decorative repeats use alt="".

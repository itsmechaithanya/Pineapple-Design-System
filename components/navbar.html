Anatomy

  1. Brand — Wordmark component (“Chay :)”, Outfit Medium 22), matching my portfolio.
  2. Nav links — .nav-link in grey; the active link is ink SemiBold with a 24 × 2px ink underline.
  3. Action — Button, Primary (ink, with ↗).
  4. Toggler — 44px square button, shown below lg.

Accessibility

  • Wrap in <nav aria-label="Main">; give any second nav a different label.
  • Mark the current page with aria-current="page", not color alone — the underline and bolder weight are extra cues.
  • The toggler keeps aria-controls and aria-expanded in sync (Bootstrap’s collapse plugin) and has an aria-label.
  • Provide a “Skip to content” link before the navbar.
  • Tab order follows reading order: brand → links → button. The focus ring is never removed.
  • Toggler and links are at least 44 × 44px; inactive link grey is 5.3:1 on white.
  • The wordmark is real text, so screen readers read it without alt text; it links home.

Accessibility

  • Each step is an <article> with a real H3 under the section’s H2. The big numeral is hidden from screen readers; a visually hidden “Step 1:” is read instead.
  • For the project card, the whole card is one link with aria-labelledby on its title — no nested links. The inner “button” is a visual cue only (aria-hidden).
  • Decorative cover images use alt="".
  • Step body text is Grey 500 on white (5.3:1).
  • Hover shows an underline rather than motion, so nothing moves for users who prefer reduced motion.
